Defensive Strength: Clean Sheets

A clean sheet is a game where a team doesn't concede at all. It's the single clearest sign of defensive solidity — Serie A title winners are almost always among the league's clean-sheet leaders, in a league famous for its defensive tradition (catenaccio).
